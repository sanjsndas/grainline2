    • About Grainline

    We are an independent editorial publication for design thinkers — architects, interior designers, and anyone who believes the built environment shapes how we live, feel, and connect.

    Founded in 2021, Grainline · House Notes began as a small newsletter shared among a handful of architects and craftspeople. It was a response to what we saw as a gap: design journalism that was either too shallow (trend roundups, clickbait galleries) or too academic (dense theory with little practical application). We wanted something in between — thoughtful, deeply researched, but always grounded in the realities of building and living.

    Today, Grainline reaches over 85,000 readers each month. We remain proudly independent and reader-supported. We do not accept sponsored content, and we do not allow advertisers to influence our editorial decisions. Every article we publish is written by someone who has spent time in the field — measuring, sketching, or making.

    Our Editorial Principles

    • Depth over speed. We take time to report, test, and fact-check. If a topic deserves 3,000 words, we’ll write 3,000 words.
    • Practical intelligence. Every essay or guide should leave you with something actionable — a dimension to remember, a material to consider, a question to ask.
    • Critical perspective. We celebrate good design, but we also ask hard questions about sustainability, affordability, and who gets to participate in shaping our environments.
    • Material honesty. We favor materials and methods that age gracefully, can be repaired, and tell the truth about what they are.
    • No sponsored content. Ever. Our recommendations come from experience, not commission.

    How We Work

    Every article begins with a question. Sometimes it’s practical (“What’s the ideal depth for a kitchen island?”), sometimes it’s philosophical (“Why do we crave patina in an age of scratch-resistant surfaces?”). We then research, interview, and — whenever possible — visit real projects. We photograph details, measure spaces, and talk to the people who live and work in the buildings we write about.

    Our guides are tested. Our essays are edited for clarity, not jargon. And we always disclose when something is an opinion, a case study, or a report from the field. You can read more about our process on our contact page.

    Independence & Funding

    Grainline is funded entirely by reader subscriptions and one-time donations. We do not run display advertising, and we do not accept payment for coverage. This model allows us to write about what matters, not what sells. If you value independent design journalism, please consider supporting us.
