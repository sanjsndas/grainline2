    How We Organise Our Content

    We keep our taxonomy intentionally small. Instead of dozens of overlapping tags, we use eight core categories. This helps you browse with clarity and helps us maintain a coherent editorial focus. Many articles touch on more than one category — for example, a piece on lime plaster might appear under both Materials and Renovation. We always assign a primary category so you can find what you need.

    If you have a suggestion for a topic we should cover, or if you're unsure where to start, get in touch. We're always happy to point you toward the right reading.
