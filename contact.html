    Before you write

    Do you accept sponsored posts or guest articles?
    No. Grainline is reader-supported and does not publish sponsored content or paid guest posts. We occasionally accept unsolicited pitches from design professionals, but editorial is independent.
    How can I submit a correction or update?
    Please email editor@grainline.house with the article title and the correction details. We review all requests and update articles as needed.
    I’d like to republish a Grainline article. What are the terms?
    We welcome inquiries about syndication. Please contact us with the article title, your publication, and intended use. We typically allow excerpts with attribution and a link back to the original.
