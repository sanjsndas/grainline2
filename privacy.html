    • Privacy Policy

    Last updated: March 12, 2025  ·  Effective: March 12, 2025

    At Grainline · House Notes ("we", "us", "our"), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and how you can manage your data.

    In short: We collect only what we need to run our editorial site and newsletter. We never sell your data. You can request deletion at any time.

    1. Information We Collect

    We collect information in two ways: information you provide directly, and information collected automatically as you browse.

    Information you provide

    • Newsletter signup: When you subscribe to Grainline Notes, we collect your email address and, optionally, your first name.
    • Contact form: If you use our contact form, we collect your name, email address, subject, and message content.
    • Comments and feedback: If we enable comments on an article, we collect the name and email address you provide, along with your comment.

    Information collected automatically

    • Log data: Like most websites, our servers automatically log standard information such as your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring pages, and timestamps.
    • Cookies and similar technologies: We use cookies to remember your preferences and to understand how our site is used. See our Cookies Policy for details.
    • Analytics: We use privacy-respecting analytics to measure aggregate traffic patterns. We do not track individual users across third-party sites.

    2. How We Use Your Information

    We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

    • To send you the Grainline Notes newsletter (if you subscribed).
    • To respond to your inquiries, story pitches, or feedback.
    • To improve our editorial content and site experience.
    • To monitor for spam, abuse, or security threats.
    • To comply with legal obligations, if applicable.

    We do not use your personal information for automated decision-making or profiling.

    3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

    If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), our legal basis for processing your personal data is:

    • Consent: For newsletter subscriptions and non-essential cookies.
    • Legitimate interest: For responding to inquiries, improving our site, and ensuring security.
    • Legal obligation: Where we are required to retain or disclose information by law.

    4. Sharing Your Information

    We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share limited data with trusted third-party service providers who help us operate the site, such as:

    • Email delivery providers: To send our newsletter (e.g., a privacy-compliant email service).
    • Analytics providers: To understand aggregate site usage.
    • Hosting and infrastructure providers: To store data and keep the site running.

    These providers are contractually required to protect your data and may only use it for the services they provide to us.

    We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Grainline, our readers, or others.

    5. Data Retention

    We keep your personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy:

    • Newsletter subscribers: Until you unsubscribe or request deletion.
    • Contact form messages: Up to 24 months, so we can reference past conversations.
    • Server logs: Typically 90 days, unless needed for security investigations.

    6. Your Rights

    Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

    • Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
    • Correction: Request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
    • Deletion: Request that we delete your personal data.
    • Restriction: Request that we limit how we process your data.
    • Portability: Request a machine-readable copy of your data.
    • Objection: Object to our processing based on legitimate interests.
    • Withdraw consent: If processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

    To exercise any of these rights, please email us at privacy@grainline.house. We will respond within 30 days.

    7. Cookies and Tracking

    We use essential cookies to make the site work, and optional analytics cookies to understand how readers use our content. You can control cookies through your browser settings. For more details, see our Cookies Policy.

    8. Third-Party Links

    Our articles may link to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

    9. Children's Privacy

    Grainline is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

    10. International Data Transfers

    Our servers are located in the United States. If you access our site from outside the U.S., your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the U.S. We take steps to ensure your data receives adequate protection, including using standard contractual clauses where required.

    11. Changes to This Policy

    We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If we make significant changes, we will notify newsletter subscribers by email or post a notice on our homepage.

    12. Contact Us

    If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact:
